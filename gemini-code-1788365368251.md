# ITINERA — India, Your Way

A season-smart travel operating system designed to help travelers discover destinations across India, estimate budgets, research through curated travel films, and build 1–7 day tailored itineraries.

Created by **Rahul Mahto**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Season-Aware Intelligence Engine**
   - Adapts UI, hero atmosphere, and curated destination recommendations according to the active month (e.g., September Green Season).
   - Live canvas particle effects (Monsoon Rain toggleable in the sidebar).

2. **Dark & Light Mode Architectures**
   - Dark Mode: Charcoal/Navy background with vibrant Lime Green (`#CCFF00`) and Warm Orange accents.
   - Light Mode: Warm Ivory (`#F8F7F2`) background with Deep Forest Green (`#1B4D3E`) accents.

3. **Dynamic 1–7 Day Trip Planner**
   - Select destination, trip duration, pace, and budget tier to generate step-by-step itinerary roadmaps.

4. **Complete India Atlas**
   - Direct destination coverage for all 36 States and Union Territories.

5. **Integrated YouTube Travel Films**
   - Dynamically search and watch destination videos directly through curated media links.

6. **Local Persistence (Saved Shortlist)**
   - Save favorite destinations to `localStorage` for offline planning.
